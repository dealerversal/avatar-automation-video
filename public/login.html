🔑 Import Google Session Data
💡 How to get your Google Cookies in 10 seconds:
  1. Open labs.google/fx/tools/flow in your desktop browser while logged in with your Google account.
  2. Open Chrome DevTools (Press F12 or Cmd + Option + I) → Go to Network tab.
  3. Refresh the page, click on any network request to labs.google, right-click → Copy → Copy request headers or copy the Cookie: value.
  4. Paste it in the box above and click Save & Connect Session!
