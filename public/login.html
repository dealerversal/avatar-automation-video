📖 Step-by-Step Guide: How to Get Google Session Cookies

  1. Open labs.google/fx/tools/flow while logged in with your Google account.
  2. Press F12 or Cmd + Option + I → Network tab.
  3. Refresh page → Click any request to labs.google.
  4. Right-click → Copy → Copy request headers (or copy the Cookie: header value).
  5. Paste in the box above and click Save & Connect Session.
